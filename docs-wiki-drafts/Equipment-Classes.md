# Equipment Classes (changes)

## Upgrading

Each class upgrade at the Class Table costs souls **and Shell Shards**, a boss material:

| Target level | Souls | Shell Shards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 10 | 1 |
| 3 | 30 | 1 |
| 4 | 90 | 2 |
| 5 | 270 | 2 |

Every boss leaves **2 Shell Shards** to each player who fought it.

## Class effects now in the game

- **Reaper:** life steal, and a share of extra souls from every kill (5% per piece, more with the level). Even creatures worth no soul may yield one.
- **Prowler:** movement speed, and a chance to dodge a blow (2.5% per piece, more with the level, at most 30%). A dodge shows a puff of air and "Dodged".
- **Guardian:** armor and knockback resistance, plus one extra armor layer, half as sturdy again (see [Nesting Systems](Nesting-Systems)).

Gear from other mods or vanilla now shows its class and layers in its tooltip too.
