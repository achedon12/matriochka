# Karma (changes)

Karma runs from -100 to +100. Purifying at the Soul Altar raises it, devouring lowers it, and so do the karma quests.

## Tiers

| Karma | Attitude | Prices | What opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +50 and more | Very welcoming (villagers glow green) | -10% | Mutual-help quests |
| +25 to +49 | Friendly | -10% | |
| -24 to +24 | Neutral | normal | |
| -25 to -49 | Wary (villagers smoke) | +10% | Black markets |
| -50 and less | Hostile in places: one villager in three turns you away | +10% | Pact quests |

Prices change both for the peoples of the worlds and for **Surface villagers** (on top of their own reputation).

## Killing an innocent

Killing a villager or one of the peoples of the worlds costs 5 karma (less once it becomes a habit).

## Black markets

From karma -25, every people keeps other goods under the counter: diamond fragments before their time, boss offerings, materials of the next world. Each black-market offer can be bought three times.

## Karma Paths quests

A chapter after the ten worlds in the Journal's quest tab.

| Path | Quest | Objective | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Help (+50) | A Light for Others | Free 100 souls at the Soul Altar | Chance Doll |
| Help (+50) | Many Hands | Trade 10 times with the peoples | 40 souls |
| Help (+50) | A Warm Hearth | Gather 16 Wool Threads | Soul Cradle |
| Pact (-50) | Hunger | Absorb 100 souls at the Soul Altar | 60 souls |
| Pact (-50) | The Price of Silence | Buy 3 goods on a black market | 2 Shell Shards |
| Pact (-50) | No Witnesses | Silence 3 innocents | 2 Diamond Fragments |

Completing a help quest adds 3 karma, a pact quest takes 3.

## Diamonds and villagers

Until your group has beaten World 5 (with progression gates on), villager offers that involve diamonds (diamonds, diamond tools and armor) are put aside. They come back as soon as a player whose group has beaten World 5 trades with that villager.
