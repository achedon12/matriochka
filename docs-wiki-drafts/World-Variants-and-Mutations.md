# World Variants and Boss Mutations

## Choosing a variant

A Seed or a rite site (see [The way into each world](The-Way-Into-Each-World)) hatches into a variant of its world. The variant is decided at the moment it becomes a World Block, from three things:

1. **The planter's karma.** At +100 the world is **Hallowed**, at -100 it is **Cursed**. Karma overrides everything else.
2. **What it was fed.** Use an item on the planted Seed or the rite site while it waits or hatches. Each offering moves its lean by one (the action bar shows it):
   - calm: any flower, Honey Bottle, Sweet Berries, Glow Berries, Moss Block
   - wild: Gunpowder, Blaze Powder, Rotten Flesh, Bone, Spider Eye, Magma Cream
3. **What lies within 3 blocks of it.** Flowers, leaves, moss and water lean it calm (up to 3 points); fire, soul fire, soul sand or soil, wither roses and lava lean it wild (up to 3 points).

A total lean of 2 or more towards one side gives the **Calm** or **Wild** variant; anything less leaves the world **Plain**. Only the planter's group can feed its Seed or rite site. A Storm Essence given to the Storm Rod opens World 4 rather than counting as an offering.

## What a variant changes

A variant never changes the terrain. It changes the creatures, the rule and the drops of that group's world.

| Variant | Rule strength | Creature health and damage | Extra drop chance | Natural spawns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plain | x1 | x1 | none | x1 |
| Calm | x0.75 | x0.85 | none | x0.7 |
| Wild | x1.25 | x1.25 | 50% | x1.5 |
| Hallowed (karma +100) | x0.8 | x1 | 35% | x0.85 |
| Cursed (karma -100) | x1.3 | x1.4 | 100% | x1.6 |

Rule strength scales the toxin, heat, noise and oxygen gauges and the strength of the gusts. The extra drop chance is a second roll of a creature's common drop and a better chance at its rare one. Bosses take the variant's danger too.

Each world has its own names for its calm and wild variants:

| 2 | Cooling Veins | Erupting Veins |
| 3 | Drowsy Dungeon | Grinding Dungeon |
| 4 | Becalmed Archipelago | Thundering Archipelago |
| 5 | Glimmering Deep | Starving Deep |
| 6 | Steady Realm | Spinning Realm |
| 7 | Slack-Tide Ocean | Crushing Ocean |
| 8 | Lingering Seasons | Racing Seasons |
| 9 | Dozing Ground | Heaving Ground |

The Journal's Worlds tab marks every world that has a variant with a small seal (green for calm and hallowed, red for wild and cursed); hover the line to read it.

## Boss mutations

From World 6 on, the boss of a wild, hallowed or cursed world wakes mutated. A mutation adds to the fight without replacing it, and its name follows the boss's in the boss bar.

| Mutation | Comes from | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Echoing | Wild World 6 and 9 | Every big attack is followed by a second shockwave a moment later |
| Frenzied | Wild World 7 and 10 | Below half health it heals a little, then moves and attacks faster |
| Searing | Wild World 8 | Its attacks set you on fire; a ring of flames follows every big attack |
| Radiant | Hallowed worlds | Every 15 seconds a pulse of light blinds, slows and outlines those around it |
| Hollow | Cursed worlds | Every 20 seconds two creatures of its world come out of its shell |

A mutated boss drops one or two **Mutated Scales** (an epic gem worth 30 souls at the Altar) and two more Shell Shards.
