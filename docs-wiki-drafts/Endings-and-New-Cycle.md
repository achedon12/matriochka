# Endings and the New Cycle

## The three endings

| Ending | How | Lasting consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sealed | Beat the Unfinished with a karma of 0 or more | Two more hearts, for good |
| Absorbed | Beat the Unfinished with a negative karma | Your strikes deal 25% more damage, but your karma falls to -100 and can never rise again |
| Set Down the Tools | Finish [World 0](World-0) | The Blank Scroll |

Every player present when the Unfinished falls gets the ending of their own karma. Each ending plays a short crawl on the Artisan's Scroll, then the credits (Escape closes it), and grants its advancement in the **Endings** tab, which appears when the Unfinished falls. Set Down the Tools is also the hidden achievement of World 10.

Endings are kept forever: you can see all three over several cycles. The Objective page of the Journal lists the endings you have seen.

## New Cycle

Once you have reached an ending and your group has beaten the Unfinished, the Objective page of the Journal shows a **New Cycle** button (click it twice to confirm). Any member of the group can start it.

| Kept | Reset |
| --- | --- |
| Souls and karma | The group's progression: every boss, the Guardian included |
| Journal pages, quests, achievements, titles | The worlds: the group gets fresh ones, new and whole |
| Endings and their lasting consequences | World Blocks of the old cycle crumble when used |
| Gear and its classes, the hidden dolls | Cores, Seeds and rite objects, World Soils, offerings, the Key of the Last Doll and Cracked Dolls are taken back |
| | The layers of the Doll of Life |

Members who are away catch up with the cycle at their next login.

In every new cycle, creatures and bosses have **+50% health** and **+25% damage** per cycle (cycle 2: x1.5 and x1.25, cycle 3: x2 and x1.5...), on top of the difficulty settings. The cycle number is shown in the Journal.

## Commands (operators)

- `/matriochka ending <seal|absorb|tools>` plays an ending.
- `/matriochka cycle show` tells the group's cycle; `/matriochka cycle begin` starts the next one.
