# Balance

Generated by `python3 tools/balance.py --baseline v0.4.0 --label 0.4.0 --write docs-wiki-drafts/Balance.md`. Each value reads "0.4.0 -> now" when the balance pass changed it.

The script reads the creatures, bosses and gear of `tools/content.py` and the moves of `tools/attacks.py`, applies the rules the code applies (armor formula, class bonus, creature and boss armor, the boss attack cycle with its openings) and compares the result with the targets of the design: the reference creature (20 health, 5 damage) and boss (500 health, 12 damage for a strong hit) scaled by x1.35 health and x1.20 damage per world, the hits of "Équilibrage du joueur" and the soul budget of 300 per world.

## Assumptions

- The player wears the full set of the world and a Berserker weapon of that world at the class level allowed on arrival (level 2 from World 3, 3 from World 5, 4 from World 7, 5 from World 9), and swings at full charge (1.6 attacks per second plus the Berserker speed).
- World 7 replaces the helmet by a Cobalt Snorkel Helmet. The Surface player has iron gear.
- In a boss fight a player hits every swing during the openings (x1.5 damage) and 60% of the swings otherwise (x0.75), none during the phase changes. Boss armor 8, creature armor 2 (elites 6).
- Creature combat time: kills the quests ask for, plus about 107 kills for the gear's resource (16 drops at 15%), 10 seconds each to reach the creature, then the solo boss fight. Exploration, crafting and travel are not counted: the design's 60 to 80 hours for a first game are mostly those.
- Souls: the plan of `design/regles.txt` (boss 100, chests 10 x 10, elites 10 x 5, altar 5 x 10) with the real quest rewards. Structure chests give souls since 0.4.0 (Soul Shards and Soul Vessels, about 100 per world, Rewards.java); they are counted as planned.

## Talents

Each class's talents open on arrival in a world (tier gates: the Guardian, then the bosses of Worlds 2, 5 and 8), bought and active on top of the design player. Attribute talents change the stats; the others are second blows of a share of the damage dealt, through the armor again (Talents.strike): the crit talents on a foe's first blow, the execute ones under a quarter of its health, rage on 25% of the blows (below half health), sneak on 50% of a Prowler's blows. Last stand heals back its share of a blow under 30% health (at most 60%), dodge is capped at 30%. The check: each measure within 25% of the design player without talents, a solo boss fight shortened by 25% at most, and the reference creature's hits to kill in the design's band. "Boss bonus" is what the tooltips add up against a boss: the weapon's class level (Berserker), then the damage and boss talents.

## What the pass changed

- The talents are now part of the check: for every class and world, the talents open on arrival (the gates of TALENT_TIERS), applied as Talents.java applies them, on top of the design player.
- Berserker: the boss talents Giantkiller and Shell Splitter 6% + 12% -> 3% + 5%, Heavy Hand and Cleaver 3% + 5% -> 3% + 4% damage, Bloodied and Red Mist 15% + 25% -> 10% + 12% below half health, Opener 20% -> 10%, Finisher 20% -> 10%, Quick Arm and Flurry 5% + 10% -> 3% + 4% attack speed. With the weapon's +58% at level 5, the whole tree cut the solo boss fights of Worlds 9 and 10 by 40% and brought the reference creature of World 9 to 3.2 hits (design 4.7); now 23% to 24% and 3.5 hits.
- Guardian: the bulwark gives health and toughness instead of armor (Stout +1 armor -> +1 max health, Plated +2 armor -> +0.5 toughness, Unbroken +2 -> +0.5 toughness, Last Wall 25% -> 10%), Broad Back and Hearty +2 and +4 -> +1 and +1 max health, Lacquer Skin 4 -> 2 absorption. Near the armor formula's cap every armor point takes about a fifth of the damage away: the tree made a World 9 player survive 5.3 strong boss blows instead of 3 (+74%), now +24%.
- Survivor: Thick Skin and Iron Heart +2 and +4 -> +1 and +2 max health, Stubborn and Never Down 15% + 30% -> 10% + 15% (+43% strong boss hits in World 9, now +18%).
- Prowler: First Cut 15% -> 10%, Stalker and Assassin 20% + 40% -> 10% + 20% while sneaking (the reference creature of World 9 fell in 3.2 hits, now 3.6).
- Reaper and Prospector talents move none of these measures; the Reaper's +28% souls from kills is shown in the soul table.

## Open points

- Talents cost Shell Shards (1 and 2 for the last two of a branch, 9 for a whole tree) from the same bosses that pay for the weapons' class levels: bringing every world's weapon to the cap leaves 4 shards by World 10, so a whole tree means leaving some weapons below the cap. A choice for the player, left as it is.
- Talents of two classes add up when gear of both is worn (a Berserker weapon and Prowler boots): the check takes one class at a time.
- Boss fights are short: with the reference boss health (500 x 1.35^(n-1)) and the design's gear, a solo fight lasts 1 to 2 minutes up to World 9 and about 3.5 minutes for the Unfinished. The design gives no length; the expertise tier asks for long fights. Longer fights would need more boss health than the formula, or a smaller damage multiplier outside the openings (x0.75, MatrioBoss.hurtServer). Left to playtests.
- World gauges (toxin, heat, oxygen, noise...) gain and drain at rates written in the Java rules, not in content.py; they are not part of this pass.
- The class column of the wiki's Difficulty formulas page is one world early (+10% in World 2): the cap is reached after the boss of the previous world, so level 2 applies from World 3, as in the design.
