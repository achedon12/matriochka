# Refuge Doll

The Refuge Doll is a pocket base for your group, in its own small dimension.

## Getting it

- Every player who takes part in the fight against the **Grand Jailer** (World 3 boss) receives one.
- A lost doll can be crafted again at the **Matrix Workbench** once your group has beaten World 3: 8 Painted Wood, 4 Sealed Fragments, 2 Gold Ingots.

## Using it

- **Use** the doll to slip inside. Use it again inside, or use the **Threshold** set in the floor, to come back exactly where you stood.
- The doll stays shut while a **boss is awake** within 64 blocks.
- The room belongs to the **group**: every member arrives in the same room. A player who leaves or is kicked from a group gets a room of their own.
- The room is a lacquered cabinet: painted wood walls between lacquer pillars, a lacquer cornice and ceiling beams, hanging lanterns. It comes furnished with a red carpet, a crafting table, a Nesting Chest, a barrel, bookshelves and flowers.
- Dig through the floor and you fall back onto the Threshold.

## Growing

| Group progression | Room (inside) | Height |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World 3 beaten | 9 x 9 | 4 |
| World 5 beaten | 13 x 13 | 5 |
| World 7 beaten | 17 x 17 | 6 |
| World 9 beaten | 21 x 21 | 7 |

When the room grows, the old walls and ceiling are taken down and new ones are built further out. Everything you placed stays where it is; the old floor border remains as a ring in the new floor.
