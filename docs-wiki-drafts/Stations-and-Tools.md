# Stations and Tools (changes)

## Matrix Workbench

The Workbench is a real 3 x 3 crafting table.

- Each recipe made there has a fixed pattern on the grid, built from its exact cost: each ingredient fills a symmetric group of cells, and the rarest (a core, for example) takes the centre.
- The result shows in a result slot. Take it, or shift-click it, like a crafted item.
- The recipe drawers act as a recipe book: pressing one lays the pattern out on the grid from your inventory.

The other stations still craft from the inventory when you click a recipe.

## Models and screens

- The thirteen stations are sculpted 3D models. They face the player who places them and have moving parts.
- Every station screen has its own layout: jars on two shelves (Spore Vat), crucibles (Ash Foundry), key tags on chains (Jailer's Table), plates in two arcs over an anvil (Lightning Forge), rune circles (Dark Altar), mirrored rows (Inverter), a honeycomb of portholes (Abyssal Press), medallions round a gear (Time Workshop), strata (Terrain Anchor), banners (Final Loom).
- A bar shows the recipe under the cursor.
- Each station has a live instrument: the Spore Vat's porthole, the Ash Foundry's thermometer, the Lightning Forge's charge cells, the Dark Altar's eye that opens in darkness, the Abyssal Press's depth gauge, the Time Workshop's clock, the Terrain Anchor's seismograph.
- The automation funnel shows on a recipe under the cursor; it used to sit on each drawer.

## Lightning Forge (World 4)

The forge needs a charge: a lightning strike under open sky, or **Storm Essence**.

- Hold Storm Essence against the forge to feed it: one essence gives 2 charges (up to 8).
- Crafting with an empty forge burns a Storm Essence from your inventory automatically. The forge shows as ready when you carry one.

## Spore Vat (World 1)

The result depends on how long the vat has fermented since its last brew:

| Stage | Time | Antidotes, dishes and other brews |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh | under 1 min | normal yield |
| Ripe | 1 to 5 min | half as much again |
| Aged | 5 min and more | twice as much |

The Filter Mask still needs at least a minute of fermentation. Gear is not brewed: it always comes out one piece.

## Inverter (World 6)

Two sockets in the niche of the cabinet. Place two pieces of the **same kind** (two armor pieces, two weapons or two tools) and press **Invert**:

- they trade their **class**, **class level** and **enchantments**;
- the flaw: each loses a quarter of its durability, counts one more class change (Chance Doll rerolls cost more), and an enchantment the other piece cannot carry is lost.

The Inverter's recipes are still there, in its mirrored rows.

## Drill (World 2)

- Mines a **3 x 3 square** across the face you look at.
- Every square burns fuel: one **Blazing Rock** (taken from your inventory when the tank is empty) feeds 8 squares.
- Without fuel, or when sneaking, it mines one block like a pickaxe.
- The tooltip shows the fuel left.
