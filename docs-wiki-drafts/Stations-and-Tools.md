# Stations and Tools (changes)

## Lightning Forge (World 4)

The forge needs a charge: a lightning strike under open sky, or **Storm Essence**.

- Hold Storm Essence against the forge to feed it: one essence gives 2 charges (up to 8).
- Crafting with an empty forge burns a Storm Essence from your inventory automatically. The forge shows as ready when you carry one.

## Spore Vat (World 1)

The result depends on how long the vat has fermented since its last brew:

| Stage | Time | Antidotes, dishes and other brews |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh | under 1 min | normal yield |
| Ripe | 1 to 5 min | half as much again |
| Aged | 5 min and more | twice as much |

The Filter Mask still needs at least a minute of fermentation. Gear is not brewed: it always comes out one piece.

## Inverter (World 6)

Two sockets in the niche of the cabinet. Place two pieces of the **same kind** (two armor pieces, two weapons or two tools) and press **Invert**:

- they trade their **class**, **class level** and **enchantments**;
- the flaw: each loses a quarter of its durability, counts one more class change (Chance Doll rerolls cost more), and an enchantment the other piece cannot carry is lost.

The Inverter's recipes are still there in the drawers.

## Drill (World 2)

- Mines a **3 x 3 square** across the face you look at.
- Every square burns fuel: one **Blazing Rock** (taken from your inventory when the tank is empty) feeds 8 squares.
- Without fuel, or when sneaking, it mines one block like a pickaxe.
- The tooltip shows the fuel left.
