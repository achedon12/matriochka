# World 10: The Giant Workshop at doll scale

World 10 is the Artisan's workshop seen by a doll. On its felt floor lie things many times your height. They are scenery: no chest, no page, and they do not count for the "visit the structures" achievements.

| Prop | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Giant Spool | A wooden spool wound with coloured thread, standing or on its side, its loose end trailing on the floor |
| Giant Nail | A nail driven into the floor at a slant, its head a platform, or one lying on its side; a little rust |
| Giant Pin | A dressmaker's pin with a round coloured head |
| Giant Button | A flat button with four holes, sometimes still sewn through |
| Giant Thimble | A dimpled thimble with a dent you can walk in by, lit inside |
| Wood Offcuts | Boards cut off the Artisan's work, lying across each other under felt scraps |
| Giant Scissors | A pair of scissors left open |
| Giant Pencil | A sharpened yellow pencil with its eraser |

## The Titanic Workbench and the Maquette

The Titanic Workbench is a carpenter's bench as tall as a house: four great legs, a 33-block top reached by a ladder up one leg, the Artisan's hammer, saw, rule, spool and nails on it, and hidden chests in its drawer, on the spool and under it.

At the centre of its top, a birch board in a gold frame holds the **Maquette**: a scale model of your own base. When your group's Traveller's Matryoshka opens World 10, the 64 x 32 x 64 blocks around it (8 below, 24 above) are sampled at a quarter scale, each 4 x 4 x 4 cube becoming its most common solid block. The first time someone of your group comes to the workbench, the 16 x 8 x 16 model is built there with real blocks. Water shows as light blue glass, lava as orange glass, falling blocks as sandstone or cobblestone, chests and other block entities as spruce planks, and your World Block as gold.

A World 10 opened before the Maquette existed uses the group's first World Block, its bed or the world spawn instead, sampled from a member of the owning group.
