# Talents

Every class has a small talent tree, learnt at the **Class Table**: its **Talents** button opens a cabinet with the six classes in the lower drawer, the chosen class's tree on the wooden panel and the talent under the cursor (or the one you picked) on the red panel, with its **Learn** button. Your souls wait by the lantern in the left niche, your Shell Shards in the right one. Learnt talents are kept on the player: they survive death and follow you into every world.

## How a tree works

- Each class has **three branches of four talents**. A branch is learnt from the top down: a talent needs the one above it. Talents of a branch add up (Stout and Plated give 3 armor).
- A talent costs souls, and the last two of a branch also cost **Shell Shards**. Each tier also waits for your group's progression:

| Tier | Souls | Shell Shards | Opens once your group has beaten |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 | 0 | the Block Guardian |
| 2 | 50 | 0 | the Ash Titan (World 2) |
| 3 | 120 | 1 | the Light Eater (World 5) |
| 4 | 250 | 2 | the Master of Seasons (World 8) |

A whole tree costs 1,320 souls and 9 Shell Shards. Creative players learn for free. You must stand within 6 blocks of a Class Table.

- **A class's talents act only while you wear or hold at least one item of that class** (any tool wakes the Prospector's). The cabinet shows a glow behind the plates of the classes your gear wakes right now, and every talent's tooltip says so.
- **Forget** unlearns every talent and gives back half the souls spent on them (never the shards).

Attribute talents (armor, health, speed, reach and so on) become modifiers on the player while their class is awake. The others act on blows, kills and mining. Survivor resistance adds up to 25% on top of the gear's 60% cap, and Reaper soul gain adds to the gear's.

## Details

- "Out of combat" means no blow taken or struck for 5 seconds.
- "First blow on a creature" is the first player blow that creature takes, from anyone.
- The extra damage of Bloodied, Red Mist, Giantkiller, Shell Splitter, Opener, First Cut, Finisher, Stalker and Assassin is dealt as a second, smaller blow right after yours.
- Slip Away and Phantom roll on their own, on top of Prowler gear's dodge, at most 30%.
- Shell Gatherer rolls when a story boss falls within 64 blocks of you, never for a trial boss.
- The Lode talents work on vanilla ores and on every Matriochka ore block.

## Stored data

Each learnt talent is a player flag `talent_<class>_<id>` in the Matriochka player state; the souls spent are counted in `talent_spent` for the refund. The trees are generated from `tools/content.py` (`TALENTS`, `TALENT_TIERS`, `TALENT_EFFECTS`) into `content/ReplayContent.java`; the behaviour lives in `replay/Talents.java`.
