# Hidden Dolls

Each world hides one doll in its secret structure, in the vault chest, next to a **Carver's Note**. Gathering the ten grants the hidden achievement *Every Doll*. Nested together in the right order, they become the key to World 0.

## Nesting

1. Hold the smallest doll in your **main hand**.
2. Hold the next doll in your **other hand**.
3. Use the doll in your main hand (right click).

If the doll in your other hand belongs right around the one you hold, it closes with a click and you now hold **Nested Dolls (2 / 10)**. Keep the Nested Dolls in your main hand and bring the next doll to them, until the tenth closes: you then hold **the Whole Matryoshka**.

A doll that does not belong rattles and is refused; nothing is lost. The nest can only be built from the inside out, starting from the smallest doll.

## Hints

The order is not the order in which the worlds open. Every secret has at least three hints:

- **The notches.** The tooltip of every hidden doll tells how many notches are carved under its base. The fewer the notches, the deeper the doll sleeps: one notch is the smallest.
- **The coded pages.** Page 4 of every world's Journal says which doll holds that world's doll.
- **The Carver's Note.** Left beside every hidden doll: "Count the notches. The fewest sleeps deepest."
- **The peoples.** Show a world's people their own hidden doll (hold it and talk to them): they tell which doll sleeps inside it.
- **The Doll Peddler** on the Surface explains how to nest them when you show him a doll; the **Old Apprentices** of World 10 know what the Whole Matryoshka opens.

## Spoiler: the order

From the smallest to the largest: World 6, 3, 9, 1, 5, 8, 2, 10, 4, 7.

## The Whole Matryoshka

Use it once your group has beaten the Unfinished to open World 0. Before that, it stays shut. See [World 0](World-0).
