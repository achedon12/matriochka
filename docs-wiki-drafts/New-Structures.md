# New structures

Every world now holds an eleventh structure, built with its own materials (to merge into [Structures](Structures)). They come after each world's ten, so the Journal pages keep their places; "Visit the 10 structure types" still asks for ten of them.

| World | Structure | Role | Size | What it is | Inside |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Gatherers' Caravan | People | Medium, rare | Three covered wagons drawn up around a fire, a market stall, a hitching rail with hay; moss and spore blossoms everywhere | The Gatherers trade here, beside a common chest hidden in a wagon |
| 2 | Ember Circle | Combat | Medium, common | A ring of standing stones and lintels around a stepped pit, braziers at every stone, magma veins radiating from the altar | A guarded altar chest at the bottom of the pit, spawners under two stones |
| 3 | Gaolers' Gatehouse | Combat | Medium, rare | A walled courtyard with four round corner towers, a gatehouse with a raised portcullis, a keep with a banquet hall and a barred cell by the gate | The guarded chest at the back of the keep, spawners in the towers, a chest in the cell |
| 4 | Sky Outpost | Combat | Medium, common | A lookout hut on a stilted deck over a floating islet, signal fire, flags, a lightning rod on the roof | The guarded chest in the hut, a spawner in the supply yard under the deck |
| 5 | Hushed Circle | Combat | Medium, common | A stone circle muffled with sculk, candles instead of fire, a sculk sensor waiting | As the Ember Circle |
| 6 | Hanging Outpost | Combat | Medium, rare | The outpost on its islet, chains and amethyst hanging from its deck | As the Sky Outpost |
| 7 | Drowned Bastion | Combat | Large, rare | The walled courtyard and keep, sunk and half fallen on the sea floor | A vault chest in the keep, spawners in the towers |
| 8 | Ephemeral Caravan | People | Medium, rare | The caravan, half packed, under autumn leaves | The Ephemerals trade here |
| 9 | Restless Circle | Combat | Medium, common | A stone circle whose stones lean and fall, the floor split by a scar | As the Ember Circle |
| 10 | Thimble Outpost | Combat | Large, rare | A giant dimpled thimble turned upside down, a ladder inside up to a lookout hut on top, a giant pin stuck through its eave | A vault chest in the hut, spawners inside the thimble |

Their chests draw from the world's usual three loot tiers, so the world's new weapon can turn up in a vault chest.
