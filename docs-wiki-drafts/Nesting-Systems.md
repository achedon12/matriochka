# Nesting Systems

Something always holds something else. These systems unlock along the story.

| System | Effect | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doll of Life | Each layer survives one death | Worlds 1, 5 and 8 |
| Nesting Chests | A chest holds a smaller one, with sorting rules | World 2 |
| Nesting Creatures | A defeated creature releases a smaller one | World 2 |
| Refuge Doll | A pocket base for the group (see [Refuge Doll](Refuge-Doll)) | World 3 |
| Layered Armor | A layer protects the next | World 4 |

## Nesting Chests

Three sizes, all crafted at the Matrix Workbench after World 2:

| Chest | Rows | Recipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nesting Chest | 3 | 2 Chests, 2 Ash Ingots |
| Middle Nesting Chest | 2 | 1 Chest, 1 Ash Ingot |
| Little Nesting Chest (x2) | 1 | 1 Chest, 2 Wool Threads |

- A chest accepts only **smaller** nesting chests: a Little one fits in the two others, a Middle one only in the large one.
- Broken, a nesting chest **keeps its contents** and its rule; placed again, everything is back.
- **Sorting rule:** sneak and use a chest with an empty hand to cycle what it sorts: nothing, blocks, tools/weapons/armor, food, resources.
- When you close a chest, every item matching the rule of a smaller chest inside it slides into that chest, and on down the nest.

## Layered Armor

- **Lacquer Layers** are crafted at the Lightning Forge (World 4): 1 Celestial Crystal, 1 Storm Essence, 2 Honeycomb give 2 layers.
- Lay one on an armor piece at the **Class Table** ("Add a layer").
- The outer layer takes the wear the armor would take. When it breaks, the next takes over, and the armor itself only wears once the last layer is gone. Each intact layer adds one armor point.
- How many layers a piece holds depends on its class: 1, plus one at class level 3, plus one at level 5, plus one for **Guardian** gear (whose layers are also half as sturdy again).
- The tooltip shows the layers and how worn the outer one is.
