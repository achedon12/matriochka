# Getting Started

You wake on the Surface with the **Artisan's Scroll** in your inventory. It opens by itself the first time; use it (right click) to read it again. A lost Scroll is crafted again at the Matrix Workbench from 3 paper.

## The first-steps card

Until you enter World 1, a small lacquered card under the HUD plaque (top left) shows your next step: the step's item, its name, a one-line hint and ten pips for the ten steps. Each step is detected from what you really do, and the card slides in again with the next one. Your progress is kept per player, on the server.

| Step | What to do | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Read the Artisan's Scroll | You turn to its second page, "Your objective" |
| 2 | Find the Guardian's Sanctuary | You stand in a sanctuary. The card has a compass needle toward the nearest one and its distance in blocks |
| 3 | Wake the Guardian | You use the Guardian's Altar and the Block Guardian rises |
| 4 | Defeat the Guardian | Your group has beaten it |
| 5 | Place the Matrix Workbench | A Matrix Workbench stands within 6 blocks of you |
| 6 | Craft the World 1 Seed | You carry a World 1 Seed (Guardian's core, 16 iron ingots, 16 oak logs at the Workbench) |
| 7 | Till World Soil | A tilled World Soil (from the Guardian) is within 6 blocks of you |
| 8 | Plant the Seed by water | A World 1 Seed is planted within 8 blocks of you. It needs water within 4 blocks to grow |
| 9 | Wait for it to hatch | Your group's World 1 Block has formed (about ten seconds once the water is there) |
| 10 | Enter World 1 | You use the World Block and arrive in the Toxic Jungle |

- If you do a later step first (a friend placed the Workbench, you already carry a Seed), the card skips ahead.
- Players who already got further before this card existed skip straight to their step, or past the end.
- After the last step, the card says farewell for a few seconds; the Journal's Objective page takes over.
- **Hide or show it:** the **Guide** button on the Scroll (bottom left) and on the Journal's Objective page. Its pip is gold while the card shows. The choice is kept per player.
- The card sits under the HUD plaque and follows it.

## The HUD

- **The plaque** (top left by default, see [Accessibility](Accessibility) for its size and corner): the world's medallion, its name and its rule, the Doll of Life as three small dolls and your souls beside a lantern. In World 8 the medallion is the season dial.
- **The rule meter**: inside a world, a row of ten icons right above the hunger bar, drawn like the vanilla hearts and shanks. Each icon is 10% of the meter and fills in halves (5%). It sits with the vanilla status bars: above the air bubbles when they show, above a mount's hearts when you ride, and it hides with them (F1, creative, spectator).
- The containers flash white when the meter jumps, the icons tremble when it climbs fast, and near the threshold they shake and pulse in their danger colours (steady colours with Flashes off).

| World | Meter | Icon | Danger from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Toxin | a drop that fills with green poison | 75% (Weakness) |
| 2 | Heat | a flame with a white-hot core | 75% (Mining Fatigue) |
| 3 | The halls' next move | an hourglass whose sand runs | 90% (the move is near) |
| 4 | The next gust | a lightning bolt charging up | 90% |
| 5 | Noise | a bell | 50% (creatures come for you) |
| 6 | The columns' next turn | a feather | 90% |
| 7 | Breath (or the snorkel's tank) | a brass porthole of air (a small air tank) | 25% left |
| 8 | The season turning | a leaf in the season's colours | 90%, before summer and winter only |
| 9 | The next tremor | a cracked stone whose crack glows | 90% |
| 10 | The meter of the zone you stand in | the zone's icon | as in that world |
| 0 | Obsession | an eye | 75% (Weakness) |

## After World 1

Only World 1 grows from a Seed. Every later world opens with its own object, crafted at the Matrix Workbench from the core of the boss before it, and its own rite: see [The way into each world](The-Way-Into-Each-World). From here the Journal's Objective page always names the next way in. See [The Artisan's Journal](Artisans-Journal) for its tabs, and the world pages for each world's rules.
