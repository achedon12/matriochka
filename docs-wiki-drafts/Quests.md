# Quests

The quest book lives in the Artisan's Journal and holds 70 optional quests: 4 on the Surface, 6 per world and the 6 quests of the Karma Paths chapter. No quest is needed to open a world or finish the game.

## How quests work

- **Availability:** Surface quests are always available. A world's quests become available once that world can be opened, that is, once your group has beaten the previous boss.
- **Types:** Guide quests teach a mechanic, People quests come from the world's people, Karma quests ask for a choice, Challenge quests ask for a hard kill.
- **Progress** is tracked per player and completes automatically; a chat message and a level-up sound confirm it, and quests counted step by step show their progress over the hotbar. The reward goes straight into your inventory (or drops at your feet when it is full).
- **Deliveries** ("bring", "help") are handed over: use one of the quest's people (any person of that world; for the Karma Paths any people of the worlds) while holding the items. They take what the quest still needs, so you can bring it in several trips. Carrying the items is not enough.
- **Places** (visit, reach the top, the bottom) are checked every 5 seconds from where you stand, as the structure tracker does.
- **Crafting** counts both station crafts and vanilla crafting; station automation does not count.
- **Kills with a condition** (without a mask, without armor, near the people, without losing a layer) only count when the condition holds at the kill.
- **Karma quests** show Help and Betray buttons in the Journal. Help adds karma, Betray removes it; both give the soul reward.
- **Titles** are stored on your player and shown on the Journal's Objective page.

The Doll Peddler of the Surface quest "The Peddler" wanders like a trader: every few minutes he may turn up 24 to 40 blocks from a player on the Surface, and he moves on two days later.
