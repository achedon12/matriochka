# Code of conduct

Matriochka is a small community project. Everyone who takes part, on GitHub, on Discord or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to keep it a place people enjoy coming back to.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people whose first language is not English.
- Criticise work, not people. Feedback on a model, a balance choice or a pull request is welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you conclude.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, stay on topic in issues.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory jokes or language, in public or in private messages.
- Sexual content or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, advertising, and repeated disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks these rules, contact a maintainer privately: on Discord, message a member with the Moderator role; on GitHub, use the contact on the maintainer's profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Consequences

Maintainers may remove content, warn, mute, or ban anyone who breaks these rules, temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
