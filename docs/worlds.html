The Surface

Where it starts

You wake up on the Surface with the Artisan's Scroll and a sketched map. Progression stops at iron until you earn more: enchanting, anvils and the Nether answer only once World 2 is done, the End once World 8 is. The Guardian's Sanctuary waits a few hundred blocks from spawn. Use its altar to wake the Block Guardian.

Boss

Block Guardian300 health

It drops the Guardian Core, the Matrix Workbench and four World Soil 0. Your Scroll becomes the Artisan's Journal.

Toxic Jungle

Rule: ToxinLearning

A toxin gauge rises by 1 every second you breathe the jungle air and falls by 2 in water or rain. At 50 you slow down, at 75 you weaken, at 100 it eats your health. A Filter Mask halves the gain, Root Broth halves it again for five minutes, and an Antidote takes 50 off at once.

Opening it

World 1 SeedPlant on tilled World Soil 0. The seed wants water nearby.

Giant Veins

Rule: HeatLearning

Heat climbs by 1 per second within three blocks of lava and by 0.5 below y 40. Higher up and away from lava it drops by 1, in water by 2. Past 50 you get hungry, past 75 your pickaxe drags, at 100 you burn. Each piece of the Thermal set cuts the gain by 12.5%, Cooling Water takes 50 off, Ice Bread slows it for five minutes.

Opening it

Rule: LocksLearning

There is no surface and no sky, only dungeon. Sealed doors give way to Dungeon Keys, made at the Jailer's Table once you have reproduced the lock pattern. Every five minutes the halls rearrange themselves, with a ten-second warning. Light is scarce, so bring your own.

Storm Archipelago

Rule: WindMastery

Floating islands under a permanent storm. Every 30 seconds a gust hits anyone under the open sky unless they crouch or take shelter, and you hear it two seconds early. Every 45 seconds lightning marks a spot near exposed players with sparks, then strikes three seconds later. Elytra stay grounded here until the Master of Seasons falls: the Glider and the Grappling Hook are how you cross.

Lightless Deep

Rule: NoiseMastery

No light, and a noise gauge. Sprinting adds 4 per second, walking 0.6; standing still removes 1.5, sneaking 3. Above 50, every creature within 28 blocks comes for you. Darkness washes over your sight every ten seconds unless you carry a lit Solar Lantern (20 minutes of light) or night vision. Silence Balm wipes your noise.

Upside Realm

Rule: GravityMastery

Every 90 seconds gravity flips: you float upward for six seconds, then drift back down for twelve. A warning comes five seconds before. Gravity Boots ignore the flip entirely; a Stabilizer or a Flipped Apple keeps you steady for 30 seconds.

Pressure Ocean

Rule: OxygenExpertise

Under water you breathe from your own oxygen gauge: about 50 seconds without a suit, five minutes in the full Diving set. Surfacing refills 10 per second. Below y 110 the pressure starts to crush you; the full Diving set pushes that limit down to y 40. Oxygen Capsules and Apnea Kelp buy time, the Thruster brings you back up fast.

Hastened Seasons

Rule: SeasonExpertise

Time runs three times faster: a full day and night take eight minutes, each season two. Summer leaves you hungry, winter slows you down, spring and autumn are your windows. A Season Preserve shields you for two minutes, the Hourglass holds the clock for 90 seconds, and the Time Workshop only accepts recipes at dawn and dusk.

Restless Ground

Rule: TremorExpertise

Every 60 seconds the ground heaves: you are thrown and nauseous, and up to four loose blocks around you collapse. You get three seconds of warning. Within 16 blocks of a Terrain Anchor nothing moves; an Anchor in hand steadies you for ten minutes, and Grip Boots keep you on your feet.

The Giant Workshop

Rule: SynthesisFinal exam

You step out of the last block into a workshop built for giants, where your own base sits on a workbench like an object. The world is cut into nine zones of 256 blocks, each running the rule of one earlier world, from toxin to tremors. Every piece of old gear earns its place again.
