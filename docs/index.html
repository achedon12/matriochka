Matriochka

Every block can hold a world.

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3. Ten worlds, each sealed inside a single block and nested in the one before it. Every world has its own rule, its own creatures, its own station, and a boss guarding the core that opens the next.

How a world opens

One loop, ten times over. Each pass asks you to unlearn the last world.

  1. Wake the Guardian

    Follow the Scroll's sketch to the Guardian's Sanctuary and beat the Block Guardian. It drops its core, the Matrix Workbench and your first World Soil.

  2. Craft a Seed

    At the Matrix Workbench: the last boss's core plus resources from its world. The World 1 Seed asks for the Guardian Core, 16 iron ingots and 16 oak logs.

  3. Plant it on World Soil

    Till World Soil with a hoe and plant the Seed. Each Seed wants the soil of the world before it, and one condition: water, heat, redstone, open sky, darkness, height.

  4. Enter the block

    The Seed hatches into a World Block. Use it and you shrink inside, onto a Threshold that brings you back out.

  5. Survive its rule

    Toxin, heat, wind, noise, gravity, pressure: each world changes how you play. Its station turns local resources into the gear that answers the rule.

  6. Beat the boss, take its core

    Every attack is announced, then leaves an opening. Read the pattern, punish the opening, take the core.

  7. Go deeper

    The core is the key ingredient of the next Seed. Ten times, until the last block opens onto something much larger.

Built to be survived

Death teaches. It does not erase hours of progress.

  • Doll of Life

    A nested doll you wear. When you die, a layer breaks instead and you get back up. It gains layers after Worlds 1, 5 and 8.

  • Souls and karma

    Every item holds a soul. At the Soul Altar, purify it for karma or devour it for power. Karma moves prices, quests and the ending.

  • Gear classes

    Every weapon, tool and armour piece is born with one of six classes. Raise it to level 5 with souls, or gamble on a Chance Doll.

  • Play as a group

    Up to eight players share a group, its worlds and its defeated bosses. Bosses grow tougher with every player who joins the fight.
